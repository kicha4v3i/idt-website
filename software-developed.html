Our Services

Detailed Well Design

From the basis of well design, all the risks and critical items are engineered and addressed to finalize the well architecute to drill. iDT can perform the design work entirely and also undertake specific design requirements to ensure success in drilling. We can support with Trajectory Design, Casing Design, Drillstring and BHA design and analysis, Hydraulics & Hole Cleaning, Drilling Fluid, Cementing, Time and Cost Estimates.

ERD Well Design

ERD wells have their own unique challenges arising out of the extended high bend and high angle sections. Accurate modelling of Torque & Drag, Hole Cleaning, Wellbore Stability are vital to a successful ERD Well. iDT provides the entire ERD Well Design service to successfully drill your well.

HPHT Well Design

Due to high pressures, the margin for drilling is narrow. Moreover, the high temperatures cause other equipment and fluid compatibility challenges. The well design for a HPHT environment is challenging and requires robust engineering solutions. iDT and its team of experienced engineers have a well rounded experience in designing and executing HPHT Wells.

Offset Well Review and Analysis

It is the inception stage to develop the basis of design for a new well. A proper offset well study is critical lays the foundation for a robust well design and reliable well delivery.

Engineering Modelling

Need a specific engineering model to be done? iDT can provide any of these engineering requirements - Trajectory Design, Casing Design, Torque & Drag, Hydraulics & Hole Cleaning, Well Barrier Schematics, Rig Selection, Probabilistic Time Esimates.

Drilling Programs

iDT can write the complete drilling program for your well by collaborating with all team members involved in executing the well.

Probabilistic Time & Cost

By using statistical modelling techniques the prediction of project duration can be accurately determined by accounting for uncertainties and risks. Monte Carlo methods have proven to improve accuracy and helpful in decision making.

Feasibility Studies

Determine the limits at which your well can be drilled by identifying the minimum rig specifications. Gauge the ability of existing infrastructure to reach your targets.
